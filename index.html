Burrow Relay

Light nine relay lamps. Every switch also flips its neighbours.

FIELD MANUAL / 01

Close the circuit.

  1. Pick a lamp to flip it and its neighbours above, below, left and right.
  2. Turn all nine lamps on. Reset gives you the same board.
  3. Need a nudge? Hint names one switch on a shortest route to the solution.

How the wheel shapes play: a fresh hardware reading seeds the board. Higher RPM allows more scramble pulses. The reading is fixed when the puzzle loads, so the board never shifts mid-solve.

Practice has its own fixed board. No wallet, rewards or payments.
